VICECITYCOMPASS / ABOUT

An independent compass for GTA 6

ViceCityCompass is an English-language fan website for people following Grand Theft Auto VI before and after launch.

What we do

We organize public information about Leonida, Vice City, vehicles, missions, characters, guides and news into a searchable hub. Each page aims to make the status of a detail clear: officially confirmed, reported, community-identified, or still unknown.

ViceCityCompass is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Sources and corrections

Official Rockstar Games pages and Newswire posts are the primary sources. Secondary sources are used for context and are labelled where relevant. If you find an incorrect detail, broken source or outdated image, please report it through the project’s public GitHub issue tracker.

Advertising disclosure

ViceCityCompass uses Google AdSense verification code and may display advertising after Google approves the site and the required privacy settings are configured. Advertising does not determine whether a detail is confirmed.
