Cookie policy

Last reviewed: November 18, 2026

What is used now

ViceCityCompass currently uses browser local storage for limited functionality: dismissing the homepage disclaimer and saving interactive-map progress or preferences. These are not advertising cookies and are not used to identify you across websites.

Google Analytics 4

Google Analytics 4 is configured for aggregated measurement. Its analytics storage is denied by default through Google Consent Mode v2, and page-view measurement is enabled only after a valid analytics-consent update. The measurement ID is G-KHGZ1KKZBD.

Google AdSense

The AdSense verification script is present, but the website does not currently place manual advertising units. If ads are enabled after approval, Google may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement and personalization. Your choices may affect which advertising is shown.

Consent management

No Google-certified CMP is currently installed in the website code. Before serving personalized ads to visitors in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the site owner must configure Google’s certified Privacy & messaging solution in AdSense or another Google-certified IAB TCF CMP. This page does not itself collect consent or claim legal compliance.

Your controls

You can clear local storage, block cookies or change browser settings. Blocking local storage may reset map progress and the homepage disclaimer. Third-party websites linked from ViceCityCompass have separate cookie policies.
