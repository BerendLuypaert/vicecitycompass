GTA 6’s Leonida: the official world and cast

November 18, 2026 · Source-first original article

Rockstar’s official “Only in Leonida” material adds useful context to the setting without publishing a complete map or mission list. It confirms a broad world of regions and a criminal network around Jason and Lucia.

Jason and Lucia

Rockstar presents Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos as the two protagonists. The official character pages describe Jason’s history around grifters, the Army and drug runners in the Keys, while Lucia’s story includes prison, family pressure and a plan to change her circumstances.

Vice City and beyond

Vice City is the neon urban centre of the state, but Rockstar’s official page also points visitors toward the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers and Port Gellhorn. These names are confirmed locations; the complete boundaries, businesses, activities and collectible placement are not.

The wider cast

Rockstar names characters including Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre’Quan Priest, Real Dimez, Raul Bautista and Brian Heder. Their official descriptions establish relationships and roles, but do not prove a full mission order or every community theory about them.

Confirmed names and setting details are kept separate from estimated map positions and reported mission interpretations.
