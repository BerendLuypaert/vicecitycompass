GTA 6 music and radio

November 18, 2026 · Source-first original article

Rockstar’s official music page confirms more than a single radio station. It presents an adaptive Justice score, a wide selection of in-game radio and podcasts, and several named stations with previews.

Justice and the original score

Justice is the announced composer of the Grand Theft Auto VI original score. Rockstar describes it as adaptive music that changes with how players engage with Vice City and Leonida. The official page says the score album contains 30 tracks, with additional digital bonus tracks across formats.

Radio stations already presented

The official page presents Cocoteo, Back Country, Afro Bank FM, The Chamber, Flash FM and Dirty South Classics. Flash FM is described as a Vice City institution hosted by Robyn and Alex, while the other stations cover Latin music, country, amapiano, metal and southern rap or hip-hop classics.

Score, radio and soundtrack are different

A trailer song, an in-game radio station, the original score and the separate album are different categories. A track appearing in promotional media should not automatically be treated as a confirmed playable radio song.

Confirmed: Justice, the named stations and the official music-page descriptions. Still subject to updates: the complete playable tracklists and how every song is distributed across stations.
