GTA 6 launch platforms and editions

November 18, 2026 · Source-first original article

Rockstar’s current support information gives players a clearer picture of how Grand Theft Auto VI will be sold and where it will launch. Here is what is confirmed without filling the gaps with retailer speculation.

Confirmed platforms

Rockstar lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S as the launch platforms. The same support page says players can pre-order the Standard Edition or Ultimate Edition, while a PC release date is not listed in the official material used for this article.

Standard and Ultimate editions

The Standard Edition includes the game. The Ultimate Edition contains the game plus an Ultimate Edition Upgrade with named benefits including the ’95 Grotti Cheetah, Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers, Vice City Styles, Jason’s Safehouse Vehicles, the Ganado Retro Build and other items spread through the story.

The upgrade can also be purchased separately by Standard Edition owners through the PlayStation or Microsoft storefronts after the digital code has been redeemed. Availability and exact pricing should be checked in the official storefront for your region.

Physical and digital versions

Rockstar’s support article distinguishes physical and digital versions. The physical package contains a download code rather than a disc, while digital purchases include the Vintage Vice City Pack pre-order benefits when the stated eligibility conditions are met.

Confirmed: platforms, named editions and listed benefits. Still storefront-specific: prices, regional availability, code restrictions and final purchase terms.
