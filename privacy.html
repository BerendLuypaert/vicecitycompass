About this policy

This page explains the types of information that may be processed when you visit ViceCityCompass. It is a plain-language project notice, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance with every law in every country.

Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages and uses a custom domain. Hosting providers may process technical request data such as an IP address, browser information, requested URL and timestamp in server logs for delivery and security. ViceCityCompass does not provide user accounts and does not intentionally collect names, passwords or payment details.

Local storage

The interactive map may use browser local storage to remember visited locations and map preferences. The homepage may use local storage to remember that its disclaimer was closed. This information stays in your browser and is not sent to ViceCityCompass as an account record. You can remove it through your browser settings.

Google Analytics 4 and Consent Mode

ViceCityCompass uses Google Analytics 4 with measurement ID G-KHGZ1KKZBD to understand aggregated page visits and improve the site. Google Analytics storage is denied by default through Google Consent Mode v2. Analytics page-view events are sent only after a valid analytics consent signal is provided.

No Google-certified Consent Management Platform is currently configured in this website code. The site owner must connect Google’s certified Privacy & messaging solution or another certified CMP in the Google account before collecting consented EEA, UK or Swiss analytics traffic. This page does not claim legal compliance by itself.

Google AdSense

The site includes Google AdSense verification code for the publisher account ca-pub-5304587842682415. No advertising units are manually inserted at the time of this review. If advertising is activated, Google and its advertising partners may process device information, identifiers, cookies or similar technologies to measure and personalize advertising subject to your choices and Google’s policies.

A Google-certified Consent Management Platform is not currently configured in this website’s code. Personalized advertising must not be activated for EEA, UK or Swiss visitors until the required Google Privacy & messaging or another certified CMP setup is completed in the AdSense account.

External services and links

Pages link to Rockstar Games, GitHub and other third-party sources. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may set cookies or collect data when you visit them. ViceCityCompass does not control their processing.

Your choices and questions

You can block cookies, clear local storage, use browser privacy controls, or avoid external links. For a correction or privacy question, use the public GitHub issue tracker. Do not post sensitive personal information there.
